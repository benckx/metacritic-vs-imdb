Metacritic vs. IMDb

What kind of movies do you prefer? Are you more of a refined hipster or do you favor popular success? Where is the consensus and where is the disagreement? This dashboard explores movies where Metacritic critics and IMDb users disagree.

Metacritic vs. IMDb plotted
